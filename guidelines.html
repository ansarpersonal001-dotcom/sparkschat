🛡️ Platform Safety & Conduct

SparksChat Community Guidelines

SparksChat connects you with strangers for anonymous conversation. To keep this space safe and enjoyable for everyone, please follow these guidelines.

1

Be respectful.

No harassment, hate speech, threats, or abusive language toward the person you're matched with.

2

No sexual content involving minors, ever.

Any attempt to solicit, share, or discuss sexual content involving anyone under 18 will result in an immediate, permanent ban and may be reported to law enforcement.

3

You must be 18 or older to use SparksChat.

This app is not intended for minors. By using SparksChat, you confirm you meet this age requirement.

4

No sharing of illegal, violent, or explicit content.

This includes but is not limited to: non-consensual imagery, graphic violence, illegal substances, or content that violates local laws.

5

No spam, scams, or solicitation.

Don't use SparksChat to advertise, phish, or scam other users.

6

Use the Report and Block buttons.

If someone makes you uncomfortable or violates these guidelines, report them immediately and block them. Reports help us keep the community safe.

7

Files and voice calls are still subject to these rules.

Anonymity does not mean anything goes — the same standards apply across text, images, documents, and voice chat.

8

We may take action without notice.

Violating these guidelines may result in disconnection, a temporary cooldown, or a permanent ban from the platform.

By tapping "Start Chatting," you agree to these guidelines.
