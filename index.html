📲 Install SparksChat App

You can install SparksChat as a standalone app on your iPhone or Android phone without downloading from an App Store:

1
Safari (iPhone): Tap the Share button (box with an arrow ⎋) at the bottom of your screen.
2
Add to Home Screen: Scroll down the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen" (➕).
3
Open as Native App: Tap "Add" in the top right. SparksChat will launch full-screen from your phone's home screen!
